GiveIndia's due diligence. Blockchain transparency. AI impact.

AVP Charitable Trust is modeled on GiveIndia's gold standard: rigorous NGO vetting (legal registration, financial statements, governance checks), random unannounced beneficiary verification visits, and meta-transparency — our own financial statements are publicly available, holding us to the same standard we set for partners.

Every rupee flows through a SHA-256 cryptographic disbursement ledger. 80G receipts are generated automatically with PAN validation for instant 50% income tax exemption. Our semantic AI beneficiary matcher deploys scholarships and medical aid with vector-search precision — and our anomaly detection AI flags any fund irregularity before human review.

Why AVP?

  • GiveIndia NGO due diligence: legal registration, financial audit, governance, track record verification
  • Random unannounced beneficiary verification visits for program accuracy
  • Meta-transparency: AVP Trust's own financial statements publicly available
  • Instant 80G receipts with PAN validation — 50% income tax exemption auto-calculated
  • SHA-256 cryptographic disbursement ledger — tamper-proof, immutable, public
  • Semantic AI beneficiary matching: vector search deploys aid to verified recipients
  • Zero-leakage anomaly detection: every expenditure audited by AI before approval
