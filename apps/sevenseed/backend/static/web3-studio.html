Questions

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Your browser computes the fingerprint. Only the 64-character fingerprint goes into a signature or a transaction, and nobody can rebuild the file from it.

Which wallet do I need?

Any browser wallet that works with Ethereum-style networks, such as MetaMask, or the built-in browser of a mobile wallet app. Signing (2a) needs no funds. Recording on chain (2b) needs a small balance on that network to pay the fee.

Does a proof show who wrote the file?

No. It shows that this exact file existed when it was signed or recorded, and which wallet did it. It says nothing about who created the content or whether it is true.

Can I delete a blockchain record?

No. Blockchain records are permanent and public, which is the point. The fingerprint reveals nothing about your file, but your wallet address is visible.
