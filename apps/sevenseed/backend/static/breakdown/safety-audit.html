Daily Safety Inspection Protocol

1. Fall Protection & Perimeter Railings

Guardrails installed on all open edges exceeding 2.0 meters elevation (IS 3696).

2. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Compliance

100% hard hat, high-visibility vest, and safety boots compliance across ground crew.

3. Heavy Crane & Rigging Stability Inspection

Outriggers fully extended on load-bearing ground plates with certified sling tags.

4. Temporary Electrical Distribution & Grounding

Residual Current Circuit Breakers (RCCBs / ELCBs) active on all sub-distribution boards.
