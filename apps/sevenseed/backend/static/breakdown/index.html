Est. 2026 · AI-Driven Construction & Infrastructure

Procore Safety Hub + Autodesk Build + CPWD Gujarat. One AI construction firm.

Breakdown Factor is India's first AI-native construction company modeled on two global platforms. Procore's Safety Hub concept — our central command center for observation trends, inspection counts, incident reporting, and root-cause pattern analysis — shifts site safety from reactive documentation to proactive zero-incident engineering.

Autodesk Build's mobile-first approach powers our field team: digital drawings, real-time markups, punch list management, and RFI tracking all from a phone on-site. CPWD DSR Gujarat Schedule of Rates powers our Smart BOQ generator — auto-calculating civil, MEP, and finishing costs indexed to current Gujarat government rates.

Why Breakdown Factor?

  • Procore Safety Hub: centralized observation trends, inspection counts, incident root-cause AI
  • Procore Action Plans & Verification: customizable safety templates with checkpoint sign-off
  • Procore Helix AI: automated daily log generation, progress report summarization
  • Autodesk Build mobile-first: drawings, markups, field tasks, punch lists, RFI tracking
  • CPWD DSR Gujarat rates: auto-indexed BOQ for civil, MEP, plumbing, finishing
  • YOLO computer vision: real-time PPE compliance (hard hat, vest, harness, scaffolding)
  • ML schedule risk forecasting: weather, concrete curing, supply chain delay prediction
