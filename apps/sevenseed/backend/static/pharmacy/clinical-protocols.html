Borg RPE & MET Scale Progression

Phase I (Inpatient Acute, Days 1–5):
RPE 11–13 (Light to somewhat hard). MET: 1.5–3.0 (Bed mobility, seated ankle pumps, 50m corridor ambulation).
Phase II (Subacute Monitored, Weeks 2–12):
RPE 12–15. MET: 3.5–6.0 (Treadmill walking, cycle ergometer, low resistance bands with telemetry monitoring).
Phase III (Community Maintenance):
RPE 13–16. MET: >6.0 (Brisk walking, lap swimming, lifelong independent aerobic exercise).

Peripheral Nerve Lesion Splinting Guide

1. Radial Nerve (High / Spiral Groove Injury)

Clinical Presentation: Wrist Drop + Finger drop due to extensor paralysis.
Prescribed Orthosis: Dynamic Extensor Assist Cock-Up Splint with outrigger (holds wrist in 30° extension while allowing active finger flexion).

2. Ulnar Nerve (Low / Wrist Level Injury)

Clinical Presentation: True Claw Hand (hyperextension of 4th/5th MCP and flexion of IP joints due to lumbrical paralysis).
Prescribed Orthosis: Knuckle Bender (Lumbrical Block) Splint preventing MCP hyperextension, directing EDC pull to extend IP joints.

3. Median Nerve (Low / Carpal Tunnel Injury)

Clinical Presentation: Ape Thumb Deformity (thenar wasting, loss of opposition).
Prescribed Orthosis: Short Opponens (C-Bar) Splint maintaining first web space and holding thumb in functional opposition.

Post-Mastectomy Lymphedema Recovery

Manual Lymphatic Drainage (MLD) Protocol: Clear proximal nodal basins first (deep abdominal breathing $\rightarrow$ contralateral axilla $\rightarrow$ ipsilateral groin $\rightarrow$ affected arm from proximal to distal strokes).

Progressive Shoulder Ladder:
• Days 1–3: Squeeze rubber ball, wrist active ROM, elbow flexion/extension.
• Days 4–7: Pendulum (Codman's) exercises, wall climbing within pain-free limit.
• Post-Drain Removal: Pulley exercises, bilateral wand raises past 90° shoulder abduction.

Postnatal Diastasis Recti Protocol

Self-Assessment Finger Test: Lie hook-lying. Place 2 fingers horizontally above the umbilicus and gently lift head. If gap is $>2$ finger-widths ($>2\text{ cm}$), diastasis recti is positive.

Contraindications: Avoid standard sit-ups, crunches, and full planks which create intra-abdominal outward bulge.

Correct Progression: Focus on isolated Transversus Abdominis (TrA) drawing-in maneuver with coordinated diaphragmatic exhalation.
