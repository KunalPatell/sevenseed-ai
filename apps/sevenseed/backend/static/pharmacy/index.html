GoodRx Gold + Drugs.com 24,000 database. For India.

Decode Forest Pharmacy combines two US healthcare benchmarks for the Indian market. GoodRx Gold's model — discounts at 70,000+ pharmacies through cash-pay comparisons, telehealth consultations, and generic bioequivalent savings up to 80% — now serves Gujarat. Our Drugs.com-equivalent database covers 24,000+ prescription drugs, OTC medicines, and natural products with peer-reviewed, independent information.

Drugs.com's 4-drug side-by-side comparison tool (side effects, ratings, costs, warnings) is replicated in our Compare Medicines feature. Our pill identifier locates any tablet by imprint code, color, and shape from our visual database. Forest Ayurveda maps evidence-backed botanicals (Ashwagandha, Giloy, Brahmi, Curcumin, Tulsi) to modern clinical safety ratings.

Why Decode Forest?

  • GoodRx Gold savings model: up to 80% off branded drugs via Jan Aushadhi bioequivalents
  • GoodRx telehealth-style online consultation: doctor visit + prescription in 20 minutes

  • Drugs.com 4-drug comparison: side-by-side side effects, ratings, costs, contraindications
  • Drugs.com Pill Identifier: imprint code + color + shape visual lookup
  • Drug-Drug Interaction Checker: flag harmful combinations before dispensing
  • Forest Ayurveda: clinical safety-rated botanical remedies with modern evidence mapping
