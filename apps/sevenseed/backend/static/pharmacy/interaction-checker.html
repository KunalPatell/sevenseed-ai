⚠️ MAJOR SEVERITY INTERACTION (CLINICALLY DANGEROUS)

Aspirin + Warfarin

Concurrent use significantly increases the risk of life-threatening gastrointestinal bleeding. Both agents impair platelet aggregation and coagulation cascade. Dual anti-platelet and anti-coagulant therapy requires strict prothrombin time (INR) hospital monitoring.

⚡ MODERATE INTERACTION (AVOID COMBINATION)

Aspirin + Ibuprofen

Ibuprofen can interfere with the anti-platelet cardio-protective effect of low-dose aspirin when taken concurrently. Take immediate-release aspirin at least 30 minutes before ibuprofen.
