Questions

What is an AI key?

A key from an AI provider that lets Sevenseed's tools use that provider's AI on your account. You paste it once on the Your AI keys page; it stays in your browser.

Do tools without AI need a key?

No. Calculators, planners, checkers and trackers that don't use AI work for everyone, with no key and no limit.

What happens when I hit the free limit?

AI answers pause until the hour resets, and the page tells you. Add your own key, or ask about Sevenseed AI, to keep going.
