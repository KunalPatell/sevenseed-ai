Your complaint draft

To,
The Station House Officer,
[Police Station name]

Subject: Complaint for registration of FIR under Section 173 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023

Respected Sir/Madam,

I, [your full name], residing at [your address], phone [your phone], wish to report the following incident.

Date and time of incident: [date and time]
Place of incident: [place]

What happened:
[Describe what happened, in order, in your own words.]

Person(s) responsible: Unknown

I request you to register an FIR and take action as per law. If this police station does not have jurisdiction, I request registration of a Zero FIR and transfer to the concerned police station.

I declare that the above facts are true to the best of my knowledge.

Yours faithfully,
[Name]
Date: 11 November 2026
Signature: ____________

Your rights

  • The police must register an FIR for a cognizable offence, and you get a free copy.
  • Any police station must take a Zero FIR even if the crime happened elsewhere.
  • If they refuse, you can send the complaint in writing to the Superintendent of Police.
