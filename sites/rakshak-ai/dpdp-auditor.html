Statutory Compliance Audit Wizard

Notice & Purpose Limitation Sec 5(1)
Itemized notice stating exact personal data collected, specific purpose, and DPO grievance contacts in English & 8th Schedule languages.
Granular & Affirmative Consent Sec 6(1)
No pre-ticked checkboxes or bundled consent. Must be free, specific, informed, and unambiguous.
Consent Withdrawal Parity Sec 6(4)
Withdrawing consent is as easy as granting it, with immediate cessation of further processing.
Data Principal Rights (DSR Portal) Sec 11-13
Automated self-service mechanism for users to request summary of data, correction, completion, and erasure.
Reasonable Security Safeguards Sec 8(5)
AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, role-based access control, and periodic vulnerability audits.
Breach Notification to DPBI Sec 8(6)
Documented runbook to notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users within the statutory window.
Children's Data Protections Sec 9
Verifiable parental consent for users under 18; complete ban on behavioral tracking or targeted ads.
Storage Limitation & Erasure Sec 8(7)
Automated deletion triggers when specified processing purpose has been fulfilled.
DPO & Governance Structure Sec 10(2)
India-resident Data Protection Officer reporting directly to executive board.
