FAQ

Questions, answered

How does the Duolingo league system work?
Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Diamond leagues with weekly XP competitions. Streak freezes prevent losing your streak if you miss a day. Loss-aversion mechanics are proven to 5x retention vs standard LMS platforms.
What FreeCodeCamp certifications are available?
Five tracks: Responsive Web Design, JavaScript Algorithms & Data Structures, Front-End Libraries, Data Visualization, and Machine Learning with Python — all browser-based with instant test runners.
How many mental models are covered?
Eight core Farnam Street models with interactive problem deconstruction: First Principles, Inversion, Occam's Razor, Probabilistic Thinking, Second-Order Thinking, Circle of Competence, Compounding, and Hanlon's Razor.
What is the 100 Days of AI challenge?
A structured daily curriculum with one challenge per day, community leaderboard rankings, badge rewards at Day 7, 30, 60, and 100, and a verified certificate upon completion.
