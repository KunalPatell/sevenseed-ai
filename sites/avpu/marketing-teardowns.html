Copywriting · Value Proposition +42% Conversions

Sell the Hole, Not the Drill

❌ Before (Feature-Focused) "Our automated platform runs 40+ LLM benchmarks on distributed GPU nodes."
✔ After (Outcome-Focused) "Catch AI hallucinations before your customers do. Sleep peacefully at night."

The Takeaway: Customers do not buy tools or features; they buy the relief of emotional friction and specific business outcomes.

Growth In Reverse · Codie Sanchez 0 → 1.1M Subscribers

The Contrarian Thinking Flywheel

Top of Funnel: Short-form YouTube Shorts & TikTok dissecting boring cash-flow businesses (Laundromats, Car Washes).
Conversion: High-value free spreadsheet templates driving newsletter subscriptions.
Monetization: $2,000 community membership + private equity acquisition fund.

The Takeaway: Niche down into an overlooked sector (Main Street businesses), demystify financial mechanics with plain English, and monetize high-ticket equity.

Landing Page · Pricing Section +31% ARPU

The Decoy Pricing Effect

❌ Flat Pricing Basic: $19/mo | Pro: $49/mo
✔ Decoy Anchor Basic: $19 | Pro: $49 (Recommended) | Agency: $199

The Takeaway: Adding an ultra-premium tier acts as a psychological price anchor, making the middle "Pro" tier feel like an undeniable bargain.

Growth In Reverse · Justin Welsh $5M+ Solopreneur ARR

The LinkedIn Content Operating System

Distribution: 2 daily high-signal LinkedIn posts using proven copywriting hooks and carousels.
Retention: The Saturday Solopreneur newsletter sharing 1 actionable tip per week.
Monetization: Two $150 evergreen digital courses with 90%+ profit margin.

The Takeaway: Predictable distribution beats viral luck. Build a content operating system that recycles high-performing themes into modular courses.
