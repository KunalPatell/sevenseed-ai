FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Buyhatke LookAlike?
An AI feature that uses product embeddings to find structurally similar alternatives across 100+ stores — often at 10-20% lower price than your current selection.
What is Spend Lens?
A personal spending tracker that aggregates your Amazon, Flipkart, and other platform order histories to show monthly spend breakdowns, category patterns, and saving opportunities.
How does Xerve auto coupon work?
The system automatically detects available promo codes and bank card discounts (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis) for your selected product and applies the highest-value combination at checkout.
What is Smartprix Spec Score?
A proprietary performance metric that scores every gadget on a single number based on processor benchmark, display quality, battery efficiency, and camera specs — making comparisons instant.
