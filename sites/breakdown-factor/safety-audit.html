Daily Site Safety Inspection Protocol

1. Fall Protection & Perimeter Railings

Guardrails & safety nets installed on open slab edges >2.0m elevation (IS 3696 / OSHA 1926.502).

2. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Compliance

100% hard hat, high-visibility vest, and steel-toe safety footwear verified across ground crew.

3. Crane & Heavy Rigging Slings

Outriggers fully extended on timber mats with certified wire rope inspection tags.

4. Temporary Electrical Distribution & Grounding

Residual Current Circuit Breakers (RCCBs 30mA) operational on all distribution boards.

5. Deep Trench Shoring & Cave-in Prevention

Sheet piling / benching implemented for excavations exceeding 1.5m depth (IS 3764).

6. Hot Work & Fire Protection Equipment

Class ABC fire extinguishers positioned within 10m of all welding and gas cutting operations.
