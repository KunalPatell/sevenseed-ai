How we work

From seed to forest, in four stages.

  1. 01

    Find the bottleneck

    We study real workflows and look for painful, expensive steps where AI changes the economics.

  2. 02

    Plug into the stack

    New ventures start on our existing agents, vector search and model gateway.

  3. 03

    Ship in 90 days

    Studio engineers pair with domain founders to get a production MVP live inside 12 weeks.

  4. 04

    Compound

    Each venture shares tooling and lessons with the rest of the portfolio as it grows.
