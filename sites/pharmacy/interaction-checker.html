⚠️ CRITICAL SEVERITY CONTRAINDICATION (HIGH CLINICAL RISK)

Aspirin (75mg) + Warfarin (5mg)

Concurrent administration significantly multiplies the risk of catastrophic gastrointestinal hemorrhage and intracranial bleeding. Aspirin permanently inhibits platelet cyclooxygenase (COX-1), while Warfarin antagonizes Vitamin K dependent clotting factors (II, VII, IX, X). Dual therapy demands rigorous Prothrombin Time / International Normalized Ratio (INR) monitoring and physician supervision.

⚡ MODERATE PHARMACOKINETIC INTERACTION

Aspirin (75mg) + Ibuprofen (400mg)

Ibuprofen competitively binds to platelet COX-1 binding channels, blocking aspirin's anti-platelet cardio-protective mechanism. If both are necessary, take immediate-release low-dose aspirin at least 30 minutes before ibuprofen, or 8 hours after.
